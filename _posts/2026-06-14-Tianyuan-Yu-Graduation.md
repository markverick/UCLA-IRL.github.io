Congratulations to [Tianyuan Yu][name] on earning his Ph.D. in Computer Science from UCLA!

His dissertation, [*Developing a Trust Plane for Interconnected Networks*][thesis], introduces a trust-plane framework that provides systematic support for authentication, authorization, and auditing in networked systems. The work brings together secure bootstrapping, trust-relationship maintenance, certificate revocation, and auditable ledgers within a unified architecture.

Tianyuan continues his work with the Internet Research Laboratory as a postdoctoral researcher. We congratulate him on this achievement and look forward to his continued contributions to the lab.
